Why these three resources matter

Helium-3

Cools quantum computers and powers neutron detectors. It is a possible future fusion fuel. Earth has less than 10 kg in reserves. The Moon has far more, because the Sun has been dusting it with helium-3 for billions of years.

Water ice

Drinking water, air to breathe, and rocket fuel. Fuel made on the Moon means not hauling it from Earth. The ice sits in dark craters at the poles, so the south pole has become the most wanted place on the Moon.

Rare earths

Needed for magnets in electric cars, wind turbines and weapons. China supplies most of the world's refined rare earths today. Moon rock called KREEP is rich in them, but no one knows yet whether mining it could ever pay off.
